Most note sites call themselves a garden and organise like a tree. This theme draws the notes as [[A Lattice, Not a Garden\|a lattice]] instead: topics one way, threads of reading the other.

The reading model comes from Andy Matuschak's working notes; see [[Reading Horizontally]]. The name comes from the Linzer torte, whose pastry lattice is the oldest version of the picture.
