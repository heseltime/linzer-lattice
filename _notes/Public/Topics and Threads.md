---
title: Topics and Threads
date: 2026-11-10
---

A **topic** is a note that starts reading paths. Add `topic: true` and it appears in the top bar:

```yaml
---
title: The Idea
topic: true
order: 2         # place in the top bar
label: The idea  # text in the top bar
emoji: 🥧        # topics only
---
```

A **thread** is what you read from a topic, pane by pane. Nothing is tagged by hand: under each title, the theme shows which topics reach the note (directly, or two links away), and the home page draws all notes against all topics as [[A Lattice, Not a Garden\|a lattice]]. The torte button in the top bar shows the same lattice as a Linzer torte.
