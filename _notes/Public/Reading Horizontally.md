---
title: Reading Horizontally
date: 2026-11-12
---

On a wide screen, a link to a note doesn't navigate away. The note opens as a new pane to the right, and the earlier notes stay in view. Older panes slide under newer ones and fold to a spine with their title; click a spine to bring the pane back.

Go back to an earlier pane and follow a different link, and everything to its right closes: the path branches from there. The open panes are kept in the URL, so a path can be shared, and the back button walks it.

The model comes from [[Andy Matuschak's working notes::https://notes.andymatuschak.org/]] and his argument in [[Why books don't work::https://andymatuschak.org/books/]] that understanding comes from what the reader does, not from absorbing a fixed line of text.[^code] On phones, notes open one page at a time.

[^code]: His notes system isn't published; this theme only borrows the idea. No code was taken.
