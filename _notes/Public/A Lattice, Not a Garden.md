---
title: A Lattice, Not a Garden
date: 2026-11-09
---

A *digital garden* brings a shape with it: beds and paths, notes growing from seedling to evergreen, and, seen through an index, a tree with a note at every leaf.

Notes rarely sort into one hierarchy. So this theme draws them as a lattice: topics are parallel lines running one way, threads of reading run across them, and a note sits where they cross.[^torte]

A lattice is also a *trellis*, which gives growth a direction without fixing its form, and in mathematics an *order* in which any two elements meet and join. See [[Topics and Threads]] for how the theme builds it from your links.

[^torte]: The Linzer torte, from Linz in Upper Austria, has the same picture on top: a pastry lattice over redcurrant jam. The theme's torte view draws your notes that way.
